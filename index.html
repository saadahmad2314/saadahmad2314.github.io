About Us — Saad Ahmad

Saad Ahmad is a free online practice platform made for school students of Classes 1 to 8. Our goal is simple: make test preparation easy, friendly and stress-free for every child, teacher and parent.

Many students feel nervous before their monthly TAB tests and school exams. Regular practice with multiple-choice questions (MCQs) builds confidence, improves speed and helps students remember what they have learned in class. This portal organises practice tests in a clean, class-wise and subject-wise way, so a student can open the portal, pick a class, pick a subject and start practising within seconds — no registration and no fees.

What You Will Find Here

  • Class-wise categories: separate tests for every class from Class 1 to Class 8.
  • Subject-wise tests: English, Mathematics, Urdu, General Science, Islamiyat, Computer Science, Social Studies and Pakistan Studies, according to the class level.
  • Mixed sample TAB test: a combined test with questions from all subjects of your class, just like the real monthly TAB test.
  • Easy English grammar & phonics tests (Classes 1–4): special short-sentence tests on Is / Am / Are, Has / Have, Pronouns, Spelling Check, Short Vowel Sounds and Comprehension, written in very easy words so young children can understand and answer comfortably.
  • Instant results: every answer is checked immediately, and a full answer review at the end shows the correct answer for every question.
  • Simple design: big buttons, large readable text and easy Urdu/English guidance at every step, made for children, teachers and school tablets (TABs).

Who Is This Portal For?

This portal is for primary and middle school students who want to prepare for monthly tests and annual exams, for teachers who want ready-made practice material for their classrooms, and for parents who want their children to revise at home in a safe, advertisement-light and easy environment.

Our Mission

We believe every child can improve with regular, small practice. Ten questions a day can change a student's result in one term. Saad Ahmad is our small effort to support quality education and joyful learning for every student.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this portal free?

Yes. All practice tests on Saad Ahmad are completely free. There is no registration and no fee.

Which classes and subjects are covered?

Classes 1 to 8, with subjects English, Math, Urdu, Science, Islamiyat, Computer, Social Studies and Pakistan Studies, arranged class-wise and subject-wise.

How does the test work?

Enter your name (optional), choose your class, choose your subject and answer 10 MCQs. You get your result instantly with a full review of correct answers.
